## Lead — first Trump–Xi–Putin summit, Shenzhen (Asia / diplomacy)

- 19 Nov: Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin held the first-ever three-way meeting of
  the US, Chinese and Russian leaders, at Kylin Villa in Shenzhen on the sidelines of
  APEC; about 90 minutes. Trump also met Putin one-on-one (42 minutes).
- Trump said the three are "uniquely positioned" to reduce nuclear weapons; Xi called the
  meeting fruitful and told the US to do "the right thing" on Taiwan; talks covered
  Ukraine, Iran and nuclear arms. No joint statement or agreement reported.
- https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/trump-xi-putin-trilateral-meeting-china.html
- https://abcnews.com/Politics/live-updates/trump-asia-trip-2026/?id=138187702

## UN top court: Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya (Asia / law)

  that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in 2016–17; the ruling is final.
